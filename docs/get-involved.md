## Change the Shape of Engineering Leadership

Senior engineers across organisations are coming together to tackle shared challenges — from collaboration and open innovation to inclusivity and real-world impact.

**Now we need more voices in the room.**

## Why This Matters

At senior levels, engineering leadership often lacks the diversity of thought and perspective needed to solve today's problems effectively.

**The Distinguished Engineer Community SIG creates a space to:**

- Collaborate across organisations
- Apply expertise where it makes a real difference
- Build more inclusive and representative leadership

## Ways to Get Involved

### 1. Technical Guidance & Thought Leadership

Help shape ideas and contribute to shared knowledge across the industry.

You can:

- Propose technical topics, standards, or areas to explore
- Write or co-author articles and blogs (via FINOS or external channels)
- Share drafts, ideas, or perspectives for peer input
- Lead or join knowledge-sharing sessions
- Suggest external publication opportunities

### 2. Practical Advice & Outreach to Community Organisations

Support real-world impact through engineering expertise — both within FINOS and in the wider community.

You can:

- Share practical insights from your day-to-day experience
- Suggest outreach opportunities or charities to partner with
- Contribute examples of impactful technical volunteering
- Help create toolkits, checklists, or guidance for community use
- Participate in Q&A sessions, clinics, or advisory formats

### 3. Mentoring & Elevate Programme

Support more inclusive and representative engineering leadership.

You can:

- Volunteer as a mentor or mentee
- Share mentoring approaches that work in your organisation
- Propose formats (buddying, reverse mentoring, fireside chats)
- Suggest matching approaches for mentors/mentees
- Help develop a shared mentoring toolkit or playbook
